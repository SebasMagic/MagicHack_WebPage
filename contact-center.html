Retention

Your churn isn't a people problem. It's an operations problem.

Customers don't leave because your agents are bad. They leave because the process around them leaks. We fix the process.

Book a diagnostic See the process
The problem

The floor is busy. The numbers aren't moving.

When a contact center underperforms, the cause is almost never effort. It's what sits between the agent and the customer.

The CRM fights the agent

Context lives three clicks away, so agents ask what the customer already told someone else. Every repeat question costs a little more goodwill.

Nobody sees churn coming

The account that leaves next month is already showing the signs today. Without a signal, the save conversation happens after the decision is made.

Training that doesn't stick

Scripts get handed out, quality gets scored, and nothing changes on the floor because nobody rebuilt the workflow underneath the script.

The process

Fix the process, then coach the people

We work on the floor before we touch a slide. What we change is what the agent actually does, minute to minute.

01
Phase 01

Listen to the floor

We sit with agents, follow real conversations end to end, and time every step. The gap between the documented process and the lived one is where the churn hides.

02
Phase 02

Rebuild the workflow

We reorganize the CRM around the conversation, not the org chart. Context up front, fewer clicks, and the next best action visible without hunting for it.

03
Phase 03

Wire the churn signals

Usage drops, silence, ticket patterns, and payment friction get turned into alerts that reach a human while the account can still be saved.

04
Phase 04

Coach on real calls

We train on the team's own recordings, not on generic scripts. Agents learn the new workflow with cases they recognize.

05
Phase 05

Hand over the rhythm

Owners, review cadence, and the metrics that matter get documented. The floor keeps improving after we stop showing up.

Deliverables

What the floor keeps

Rebuilt CRM workflow

Screens, fields, and routing reorganized around the conversation. Less hunting, more resolving.

Churn signal alerts

The behaviors that precede a cancellation, turned into alerts routed to whoever can still act on them.

Save playbooks

What to say, what to offer, and when to escalate, written from the calls that actually retained an account.

Agent scorecard

Resolution, retention, and handle time per agent. Coaching gets aimed at a number instead of a feeling.

Onboarding kit

New agents reach the floor already knowing the workflow, instead of learning it from whoever sits nearest.

Documentation and training

Every process documented and handed over. Your supervisors run the rhythm without us.
